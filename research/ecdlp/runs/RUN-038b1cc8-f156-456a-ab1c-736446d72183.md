## 2. Analysis of Lemma A transfer
The paper claim "propagation starts from one adjacent pair, so it transfers verbatim" is **incomplete**. The
original proof of `degree_bound_rational_coprime` propagates only *upward* from (N-1, N) to k = r-1 and then
uses the hypothesis `A = kB` at k = 1 and k = 2 (`hAB 1`, `hAB 2`) for the wrap-around contradiction
(x_{r-1}=x_1, x_{r-2}=x_2 gives 2 = 0). With gaps in G, 1 and 2 need not lie in G, so upward propagation
alone gives no contradiction. The repair: the identity `key` is symmetric, so from `Nrat = 0` and the pair
(n, n+1) good one also gets n-1 good (B_n B_{n+1} (A_{n-1} - (n-1) B_{n-1}) = 0 by ring, and B_{n-1} != 0 by
coprimality). Propagating up to r-1 AND down to 1 from one adjacent pair (k0-1, k0) in G yields Good on all of
[1, r-1], after which the original wrap-around argument applies to the *propagated* facts. Both directions are
needed and proved (`stepUp`, `stepDown`). Hence Lemma A holds with exactly the paper's constant 3d. Also,
G ⊆ [1,N] is not even needed as a hypothesis (centres are restricted to [2,N-1]; B(x_k)!=0 and A=kB are
only used on G; injectivity of x_k on [1,N] uses 2N<r).
