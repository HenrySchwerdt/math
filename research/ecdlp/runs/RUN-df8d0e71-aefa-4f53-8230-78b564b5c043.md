## Verdict on H per predicate
Literal pre-registered rule: for x, y, x+1 rule A holds (M<=4.3 at every size; slope about 0.01-0.02/unit ln r, vs about 0.9 for random; the 4 d^2 p/r check with d=1 fails marginally at small p because M/(p/r) reaches 4.04-4.24, so the empirical constant is C^2 about 4.0, finite-size excess <= 6%) but rule B fails (2nd moment 2.00, equal to Exp(1)'s). By the letter this is INCONCLUSIVE. Rule B was ill-chosen: the SU(2) Sato-Tate law of |2cos t|^2 has the same second moment (Catalan number 2) as Exp(1); the discriminating statistics are the maximum and higher moments. On those post-hoc statistics (replicated on a fresh seed):
- g=x, g=x+1, g=y: bounded Weil-type behaviour with limiting law matching Sato-Tate for X=2cos t (3rd moment 5, KS 0.012, support edge at 4). H is empirically SUPPORTED (post-hoc discrimination; rule B's letter not met).
- g=x^2+x+1, x*y, x^3+2: NOT bounded in the tested range; M grows with ln r like the controls, 2nd moment 3 (real) . H REFUTED for these g (at toy sizes; the growth, not the constant, is the evidence). Note x*y is real/imaginary symmetric per p mod 4 and x^2+x+1, x^3+2 are functions of x. The higher-degree contrast predicted by the PI is confirmed, though deg of y (3 as a function on E) is below deg of x^2+x+1 (4), consistent with a threshold in degree/ramification rather than just "even/odd".
- Controls: random +-1 passes (M about ln r, m2 2). Parity/hash of x behave as real random sequences (m2 3).
Leak sanity (bucket 1, 12 curves, class means of s_k over k mod 2, k mod 3, chi-square stat vs df): means 0-1.4 (df 1) and 1.7-3.1 (df 2); all consistent with chance (for x-functions mod-2 stat is exactly 0 by the symmetry s_k = s_(-k) since r is odd). No sign of predicting k mod 2 or 3 from coordinate predicates. Bounded spectrum is equidistribution-type behaviour, not a discrete-log leak.
Proof status: experimental only. No proof that the bounds hold for all p, and the bound-and-law claim for x,y,x+1 is not derived here.

## Limitations
Finite toy sizes (p <= 1.28e5, 12 curves per bucket, 48 per predicate); max statistics have noisy slope CIs (x*y slope CI is wide); p-bucket fixed in log scale, so ln r range only 7.3-11.5. The Sato-Tate identification is by moments 1,2,5 and KS about 0.012 (finite-sample noise level not computed) and is not a proof; other bounded laws with these moments are not excluded. Degrees d were assumed. Seeds, curve samples and p/r values are in the results JSON. The two controls that are functions of x are not independent of the algebraic ones. No literature lookup was made (0 of 3 web lookups used); Kohel-Shparlinski-type bound unverified.
