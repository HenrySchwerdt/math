## Cohomological setup and dimension count
Let F = Frobenius, L: E -> E, R -> F(R) − R, a Galois cover with group E(F_p) ≅ Z/r. For nontrivial psi let L_psi be the push-out rank-one lisse sheaf (étale everywhere, geometrically nontrivial). Its Frobenius trace at Q in E(F_p) is psi(Q)^{±1} (Lang), so psi(Q) = trace of L_psi (convention only conjugates). Let L_chi(g) be the Kummer-type sheaf of w^2 = g on U = E − S, S = points of odd valuation of div g (tame ramification of order 2 exactly at S). Then
sum_{Q in U(F_p)} psi(Q) chi(g(Q)) = − tr(F | H^1_c(U, L_psi ⊗ L_chi(g))).
*Derivation (GOS, Deligne):* U affine, sheaf geometrically irreducible nonconstant => H^0_c = H^2_c = 0; tame => Euler characteristic = χ_c(U) = −#S; so dim H^1_c = #S = D; local monodromy nontrivial at each point of S so H^1_c = H^1 is pure of weight 1 (Deligne). Hence |S| ≤ D sqrt(p) (matches ECDLP-8).
- g = x: S = {two points with x = 0} (pole at O has even order), D = 2. O lies in U with trace chi(1) = 1.
- g = y: S = {T1,T2,T3,O}, D = 4 (y has simple zeros at T_i, triple pole at O); exact, no correction since all four removed points are ramified.

## g = x mechanism (derivation + numerics)
Double cover D: w^2 = x(Q) of E (ramified at the two x = 0 points; Riemann–Hurwitz: g(D)=2). Explicitly D: y^2 = w^6 + a w^2 + b. The two involutions w -> −w and (w,y) -> (−w,−y) give quotients E and E'': Y^2 = X^3 + a X^2 + b^2, so Jac(D) ~ E × E''. 
- psi trivial: sum_{Q affine} chi(x(Q)) = −a_{E''} − 1 (=Σ_x chi(x f(x))), i.e. exactly the Prym trace, with −1 = O-correction. Verified 120/120 curves (`out_check_x_prym.txt`).
- psi nontrivial: because psi has odd order, sum_Q psi(Q) chi(x(Q)) = sum_{R in D(F_p)} psi(pi(R)) = trace of F on H^1(D̄, pi^*L_psi) = H^1(Ē, L_psi ⊗ L_chi(x)) (the H^1(Ē,L_psi) summand vanishes), of dimension 2g−2 = 2 (Euler char −2). Equivalently: the Z/r-Lang cover D' = D ×_E E of D has genus r+1 and H^1(D') = ⊕_psi H^1(D, pi^*L_psi) (dims 4 + 2(r−1) = 2r+2). So the sum is the trace of F on the psi-isotypic part of H^1 of an unramified Z/r-cover of the genus-2 curve D — the "Prym of D twisted by psi".
- Numerics over F_p, F_{p^2}, F_{p^3} (psi∘Tr_{F_{p^n}/F_p}), 14 curves, p = 11..31: power sums p_n = −(S_n + 1) satisfy Newton's rank-2 relation p_3 = (3p_1p_2 − p_1^3)/2 to 0.0 (rounded 1e-8) for all c, and det = e_2 = p exactly (real, =+p; `out_x_rank2_test.txt`, `out_det_phase.txt`). So the Frobenius lies in SU(2)·sqrt(p) (not merely U(2)), consistent with Sato–Tate / semicircle for |Y|^2 = (2cos t)^2. Reason for det = p (derivation sketch, not fully checked): x even => psi ↔ psi^{-1} symmetry makes Y real and the pairing self-dual.

## g = y puzzle resolution (derivation sketch + strong numerics)
Cover w^2 = y of E: plane quartic w^4 = x^3 + a x + b, genus 3, branched at E[2]; D = 4 and the 4-dim space is the psi-twisted anti-invariant part (Prym) with Z[i]-action over F_p(i).
1. **[−1] symmetry (derivation, verified):** y∘[−1] = −y, [−1]^*L_psi = L_{psi^{-1}} => S(psi) = chi(−1) conj(S(psi)): Y real for p ≡ 1 (mod 4), purely imaginary for p ≡ 3. Observed to 1e-15 for all curves. It does not reduce the rank (it relates psi to psi^{-1}) but explains the realness.
2. **E[2]-translation structure (derivation sketch, conjecture for the key step):** For T in E[2], y∘τ_T = c_T y/(x − e_T)^2, so chi(y(Q+T)) = chi(c_T) chi(y(Q)); L_chi(y) is translation-invariant for E[2] up to constants, and L_psi is geometrically translation-invariant. Hence E[2] ≅ (Z/2)^2 acts projectively on the 4-dim V = H^1. If the commutator pairing is the (nondegenerate) Weil pairing, V = W ⊗ M with W the 2-dim Heisenberg representation and M 2-dim carrying Frobenius' "genuine" action. #E(F_p) = r odd prime => no rational 2-torsion => cubic irreducible => F permutes T1,T2,T3 cyclically, acting on W through an order-3 element of Sp_2(F_2) = S_3, with eigenvalue ratio a primitive cube root of unity ζ. Therefore the eigenvalues of F on V are {ζ_1 λ_i, ζ_2 λ_i}, i = 1,2 (ζ_2/ζ_1 = ζ), and the trace is (a unit of modulus 1 up to normalisation) × (trace on rank-2 M). So Y_psi = unit × (2-dim Frobenius trace)/sqrt(p) is SU(2)-like although D = 4: the factor tr_W has absolute value 1 (not 2) exactly because F acts on E[2] as a 3-cycle.
3. **Numerical test of the structure (not a proof):** over F_{p^n}, n = 1,2,3, 24 curves (p = 11..37) the prediction S_3 = A^3 − 3AB with A = ζS_1, B = ζ̄S_2 and |A^2 − B|/2 = p holds with residual 0.0 for every c (`out_ext_field_test.txt`; g = x used as a negative control: residual up to 16 p^{3/2}, as expected since x has no such structure). Typical |S_3|/p^{3/2} ≈ 2–3, so the test is not vacuous. The test uses only S_1..S_3 and so does not prove rank-4 = 2×2 structure; S_4 (p^4 points) would give the full characteristic polynomial.
4. **Max |Y|:** over 120 random curves (p in [1000,5000), seed 20261104) max_c |Y_c| = 1.9997 (p≡1) and 1.9991 (p≡3) for g = y, never above 2, vs 2.017/2.020 for g = x (excess = O correction ≈ 1/sqrt p). Moments of |Y|^2 (pooled mean): y: 1.00, 2.00, 5.00; x: 1.00, 2.00, 5.00 for both residue classes (`out_stats_xy.txt`).
5. **Control (supports step 2):** curves with #E = 2r, one rational 2-torsion point, psi of odd order r, g = y: y(Q+T1) = −B y/(x−e1)^2 with B = f'(e1) gives S = chi(−B) S. Prediction: S ≡ 0 when chi(−B) = −1. Observed: 22/22 curves exactly 0; the 26 curves with chi(−B) = +1 have |Y|^2 moments 2, 8 and max |Y| = 2.82 ≈ 2√2 (larger than 2: the SU(2)-like regime is specific to the 3-cycle case) (`out_even_order_control.txt`).
Remark: moments 1,2,5 of |Y|^2 cannot distinguish SU(2) from U(2); for g=x the exact det = p (above) selects SU(2); for g=y the det of the M part was not isolated.
