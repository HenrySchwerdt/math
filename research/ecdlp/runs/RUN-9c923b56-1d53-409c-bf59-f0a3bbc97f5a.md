### 6. Inconsistency "constants free" vs charged: RESOLVED, the paper is correct
Lean source: SLPBound.lean and RationalSLP.lean define `Op.const (c : F)` as an operation constructor; `run`/`runVal` append one value per op, `const` included; the theorems bound by `prog.length` (the list length), so each `const` costs one step. DecisionTree.lean: `Tree.arith (op : Op F)` with `op = const c` is an arithmetic node counted by `arithDepth`, and PRF-ab954d65 notes state "constants counted". Hence in all three formalized models constants are charged one step each; arbitrary constants are free only in the sense of non-uniform choice of c in F.
Where the word "free" appears: SLPBound.lean module docstring ("constants are free") and the theorem docstring ("constants free"), PRF-58cf340e notes ("constants free"), the review REV-7f70c161 (which also records that each constant is counted in L), REV-d7bc5940 model description ("free constants and tests"), and HYP-6da71353 remark E3 ("free constants"). These are loose wording about arbitrary constants, not about cost, but they are misleading, and the REV-d7bc5940 / E3 phrasing describes a model where constants cost nothing, which is not the formalized one. The paper's statements (Section 5(i), 5(iii), limitations) match the code. Recommended (outside the paper, not required for acceptance): correct the PRF-58cf340e note and the two SLPBound docstrings to "arbitrary constants of F, each counted as one step". The REV-7f70c161 remark that not counting constants "would also be valid" is a reviewer's argument, not formalized; the paper does not rely on it.
