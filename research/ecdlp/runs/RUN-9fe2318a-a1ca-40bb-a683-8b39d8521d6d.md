### Mechanisms (one paragraph each)
**A1.** Translation by P is a rotation of Z/r with Koopman eigenvalues e(s/r). If a cheap observable f(x,y) were a sparse sum of K characters, the
sequence f(jP), j <= N ~ O(K) << sqrt r, would have Hankel rank K and be linearly predictable; the orbit f(Q+jP) is then a phase-shifted copy, and the K
phases give k via e(s_i k/r) at cost O(K). Escape claimed from the sqrt r bound: information is extracted from a short orbit segment by spectral
inference, not by collisions. Kill condition: any observable (and any dictionary) is spread over ~r characters with coefficients O(r^{-1/2}).
**A2.** Replace the uniform step walk with MH walk targeting a Boltzmann weight w(x) concentrated on r^a points; collisions then scale as 1/N_eff.
Gate: producing a sample from pi by local moves in a randomly-labelled group costs >= r/N_eff proposals; collisions after M accepted need M ~ sqrt(N_eff);
total r/sqrt(N_eff) >= sqrt r with equality only at the uniform walk. This is distinguished-point rho in other words.
**A3.** Rational maps commuting with the Lattes map L_m are Lattes maps of the same torus; for End = Z they are the x([n]). An orbit of x(Q) under [m] is
{m^j k} in Z/r; closing a cycle or hitting the P-orbit depends on k only through k in <m>; cost ord_r(m) ~ r.
**A4.** Variables: bits b_i and intermediate points x_i; ladder constraints x_{i+1} = x(2R_i + b_i P). Message passing as in statistical mechanics.
The graph is a path with p-state variables; backward halving gives 2 candidates per step, so messages are sets of size 2^j: exact inference is
enumeration, MITM message passing is BSGS; no soft information exists (leak tests E1, round 20 null).
**A5.** Coarse-grain k by k div 2^j: Q_j = 2^{-j} Q (halving is free, r odd) and look for a computable block-spin that is predictable; the only
block-spin is a bit/MSB of the integer representative, which is a hard-core-type predicate (round 20 closes the estimator version).
**A6.** Lift the curve to Z/p^2 and use the exactly linear formal-group energy psi(kP~) = k psi(P~). Canonical r-torsion lifts exist and Q^ = kP^ holds, but
reduction restricted to E(Z/p^2)[r] is an isomorphism onto E(F_p): nothing new is visible; the linear coordinate sits on the p-part.
**A7.** The graph x(R) -- x(R+P) is a path of length (r+1)/2; Laplacian eigenfunctions cos(2 pi s t/r) would embed Q at angle k. A random walk sample
supplies local patches; two patches (around P and Q) connect only through a collision, which is rho.
**A8.** Iterate (R,R') -> (2R+R', R+R') on E x E, a hyperbolic toral automorphism with exponential mixing. Bijective and linear in the hidden logs,
orbits are lattice points with known coefficients; mixing is irrelevant because the algorithm already knows the coefficients.
