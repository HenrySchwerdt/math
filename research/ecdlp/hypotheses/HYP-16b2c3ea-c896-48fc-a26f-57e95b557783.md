Estimators for MSB(k) and for k (gradient boosting, MLP, logistic, best single feature; ~160 hand features from x(Q), x(Q+-jP), bits, comparisons, carries, Legendre symbols; 8 training + 4 held-out curves per size, 12-28 bits): 0/20 model-size cells show advantage; 99% CI half-width 0.0029 at >= 2^20, upper bound <= 0.0045 at 2^28; regression R^2 ~ -0.0005, median circular error 0.250 (guessing). Closeness detector for residual R=(k-g)P (|d|<=w vs uniform, table-recognisable |d|<=16 excluded): null at w=2^12 and w=r/32 (upper bound <= 0.0039 at 2^28); w=2^8 inconclusive (control not flagged). Planted 0.05 leak detected by boosted trees at all sizes. End to end on planted control: additive-shift majority voting does not amplify an MSB oracle; a multiplicative maximum-likelihood residual-refinement decoder recovers k (200/200 at eps 0.2, 0.1; 39/40 at 0.05) with nearly r-independent cost (model within 1.5%). Same-implementation fits: rho 1.249 sqrt(r)+346, kangaroo 3.352 sqrt(w)+424 (interval beats rho only if w/r <~ 0.14). Bar to beat rho: MSB advantage eps > ~0.055 at 2^28 (free oracle) or 0.16 (7 ops per call), scaling ~C r^{-1/4} (~1.6e-4 at 2^64); closeness detection pays only for windows w >~ r^0.8 with delta ~0.23-0.4. Measured real-curve advantage is below 0.003-0.0045.
