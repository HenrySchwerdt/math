# HYP-e1959734-9325-4a54-9d99-d4535c58a11c — Legendre/parity coordinate predicates show no k-dependence

## Exact statement
For random prime-order curves E/F_p and generator P, the predicates chi(x([k]P)), chi(y([k]P)), parity of x([k]P) are statistically independent of k mod m for small m and have no detectable autocorrelation in k.

## Provenance
Origin: A6/E1 (RUN-b0ccd1bd); empirical only, 2 curves at p ~ 10^4, no multiple-testing correction. Converse direction of known hard-core-bit results. Source RUN reports: RUN-b0ccd1bd-6bbc-4f34-b347-0ade0bde13b7.

## Status
Unproved. Correctness, complexity, experiment and independent review are tracked separately; none is established by registration.
