# HYP-6aef8c30-2942-4079-8d0a-fc2ae8b6c200 — SLP barrier with division

SLP barrier with division (x-only): over a field F (2 != 0), W short Weierstrass, P of order r, 2N < r. Any straight-line program over F with +, −, ×, ÷ and constants, single input u = x(Q), in which every division is defined (nonzero divisor) at every u = x(kP), 1 <= k <= N, and whose output equals k at u = x(kP), has length L >= log2((N − c)/c') for explicit constants (expected: the output is a rational function g = A/B of degree max(deg A, deg B) <= 2^L, and a rational analogue of Theorem C gives N <= 3·deg + O(1) or similar).
