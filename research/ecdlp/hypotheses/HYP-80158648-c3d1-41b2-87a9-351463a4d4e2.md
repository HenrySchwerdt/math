# HYP-80158648-c3d1-41b2-87a9-351463a4d4e2 — Sato–Tate law for twisted Legendre sums along [k]P

## Exact statement
Conjecture (Sato–Tate law for twisted Legendre sums along a prime-order EC group). Let E/F_p have #E(F_p)=r prime, P a generator, g in {x, x+a (a in F_p), y}. For c in 1..r-1 let Y_c = p^{-1/2} * sum_{k=0}^{r-1} chi(g([k]P)) e(ck/r). Then |Y_c| <= 2 (+o(1)) for all c, and as p -> infinity (over random such curves) the empirical distribution of Y_c^2 (Y_c real for g = x+a) over c converges to that of (2 cos t)^2 with t Sato–Tate distributed ((2/pi) sin^2 t dt). For g of higher degree (x^2+x+1, xy, x^3+2) the normalized sums are instead approximately Gaussian at these sizes (second moment of |X_c|^2 ≈ 3 for real sums) with max growing within the Weil envelope.

## Evidence
ECDLP-9 (RUN-df8d0e71): g=x, x+1, y: max |X_c|^2 ≈ 4 p/r in all buckets (slope vs ln r ≈ 0), 2nd moment 2.00, 3rd moment ≈ 5.0 (Sato–Tate value; Exp(1) gives 6), KS ≈ 0.012 to (2cos t)^2 law; two seeds; PI bit-identical reproduction (PI RUN evidence). Higher-degree g: Gaussian-like.

## Proof obligations
1. Weil-type bound |Y_c| ≤ 2 for g = x (identify the curve/sheaf). 2. Equidistribution (Katz/Deligne) for the family over c.

## Status
Experimental (toy sizes). Unproved. Novelty unchecked; likely known.
