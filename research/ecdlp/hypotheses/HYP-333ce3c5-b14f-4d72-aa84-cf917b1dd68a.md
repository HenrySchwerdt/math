# HYP-333ce3c5-b14f-4d72-aa84-cf917b1dd68a — Arbitrary p-adic lifts carry no discrete-log information when #E(F_p) != p

## Exact statement
For E/F_p with prime-order subgroup <P> of order r != p, the Smart-style quantity psi(P~) = (-x/y)([#E]P~)/p mod p computed from arbitrary lifts P~, Q~ in E~(Q_p) does not satisfy psi(Q~) = k psi(P~) mod p beyond chance, i.e. it carries no information about k.
