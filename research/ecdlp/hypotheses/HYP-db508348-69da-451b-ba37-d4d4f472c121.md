# HYP-db508348-69da-451b-ba37-d4d4f472c121 — Prime-field small-x factor bases

## Exact statement
Prime-field factor-base question (B11/A5 open requirement): for E/F_p (p prime) and the factor base V_B = {Q in E(F_p): x(Q) < B} with B = p^{1/m}, the probability that a uniformly random R in E(F_p) is a sum of m points of V_B is about |V_B|^m/(m! p) (heuristic), and finding such decompositions via Semaev summation polynomials (S_3 for m=2) costs at least as much as rho at toy sizes; no choice of (m, B) yields a relation-collection cost below sqrt(p) group operations on prime fields at toy sizes.

## Status
Unproved; to be tested in ECDLP-12.
