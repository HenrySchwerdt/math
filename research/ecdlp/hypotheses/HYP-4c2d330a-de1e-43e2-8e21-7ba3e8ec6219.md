# HYP-4c2d330a-de1e-43e2-8e21-7ba3e8ec6219 — Tightness of x-only interpolation degree bounds

## Statement
Tightness conjecture for x-only interpolation: for E/F_p, P of prime order r and 2N < r, every g in F_p[X] with g(x(kP)) = k (resp. k^2) for 1 <= k <= N has deg g >= N − c for an absolute constant c (generic curves), improving the proven bound (N − 2)/3; or else there exist families with substantially lower-degree interpolants.

## Provenance
Derived by PI from the observed gap (proven ~N/3, observed N−1) in ECDLP-25/26 toy data.
