# HYP-130de9b1-6713-4602-b1e5-6ef8a7da8486 — No efficiently computable nonconstant approximate character on a prime-order EC subgroup from low-complexity coordinate functions

## Exact statement
For a prime-order subgroup G of E(F_p) and functions f: G -> R/Z from a small explicit family of coordinate functions (e.g. x/p, y/p, fractional parts of low-degree polynomials in x,y over p), the defect max_{a,b in G} |f(a+b)-f(a)-f(b)| (distance in R/Z) is near its random-function value, i.e. no member is a near-character. (A near-character would give approximate bits of the discrete log and a lattice/HNP transfer.)
