# HYP-3c665e7b-e7e5-41d2-9c9d-b687c750700c — Inventor E: S_3 relation supply alone forces cost >= 1.22 sqrt p; parametrised and CM/isogeny factor bases do not help

Status: completed; proof status: experimental; outcome: refuted.

## Question, selection rationale and inputs
Inventor E (ECDLP-49, sub-square-root relation finder): negative, no prototype (no cost model below sqrt r). (i) For fixed x3 the discriminant of S_3 in x2 is 16 f(x1) f(x3) (f=x^3+ax+b): the decomposition curve has genus 1, j = j(E), parametrised by the group law itself (affine count #E-2, brute force on 15 curves p=101..2053). Pullback to factor base x=phi(t) of degree e: genus 1 (t), 5 (t^2), 19 (t^3), 9 (generic degree-2 rational), computed exactly; e>=4 from formulas only. Genus <= 1 forces e=1 (whole F_p); a base of size sqrt p needs e >= sqrt p, genus ~3p-4p, Weil bound vacuous. (ii) Rational isogenies on prime-order groups are isomorphisms on F_p-points; CM orbits on j=0 give only Z[omega] identities (no useful relations). (iii) Finder-independent supply bound: a random-like base of size s has ~2 s^3/(3p) closed S_3 triples (measured within 2% on four bases); s relations need s >= 1.22 sqrt p, so total cost >= 1.22 sqrt p even with a free finder: ties rho in exponent, cannot beat it (m=2). Only m>=3 (S_4, supply floor s >= p^{1/3}) untested.
