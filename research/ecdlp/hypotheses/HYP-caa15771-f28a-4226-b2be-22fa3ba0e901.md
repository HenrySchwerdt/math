# HYP-caa15771-f28a-4226-b2be-22fa3ba0e901 — Homomorphic transfer is all-or-nothing on prime-order subgroups

## Exact statement
Let G be an additive group, P in G with addOrderOf P = r prime, and phi: G -> H an additive group homomorphism. Then phi(P) = 0 or addOrderOf(phi P) = r. If H is finite and phi(P) != 0 then r | |H|. If gcd(addOrderOf P, |H|) = 1 then phi(P) = 0.
