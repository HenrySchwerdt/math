# HYP-720e359e-db30-43a5-bf3a-f5b5c33964b6 — Inventor D: algebraic (S_3/Groebner) relation finding on prime-field curves costs ~p, worse than rho

Status: completed; proof status: experimental; outcome: refuted.

## Question, selection rationale and inputs
Inventor D (ECDLP-47, algebraic relation finder): negative. Index calculus with S_3 decomposition over a PKM multiplicative-coset factor base (x in g mu_d, d=2^n | p-1; small-x control) recovers k in 65/65 runs (1581 sampled relations verified; sympy Groebner matches exhaustive search for p up to 17921), but total cost exponent ~1 (0.99 [0.92,1.06]) vs rho ~0.5: D2a/rho = 11 (16 bits), 94 (20), 633 (24), 2533 (28, post hoc). Per-target finder cost ~|V|, relation ~2p/d additions, total ~p independent of d (D2b d~p^{1/3} exponent 1.075); linear algebra <0.1%. Obstacle: hash/enumeration finders cost >= p^{1/2-1/(2m)} per relation while the PKM accounting needs T < p^{1/2-1/m}; Groebner/resultant collapses to a degree-|V| univariate problem (time ~d^2.1-d^3); no subfield over F_p for Weil restriction.
