# HYP-eff2cfbb-053a-47e9-91de-a7210488b049 — Embedding-degree sparsity: at most M(M+1) bad traces

## Exact statement
Fix a prime power q and M >= 1. Call an integer t with |t| <= 2 sqrt(q) M-bad if q+1-t has a prime factor r > 4 sqrt(q) with r not dividing q and multiplicative order of q mod r at most M. Then the number of M-bad t is at most M(M+1).

Origin: B3 (RUN-fec2442f). Statement about traces only; curve-count weighting by Hurwitz class numbers is a separate unproved claim. Finite check p <= 80021 reported 1..3 bad traces vs 42. Source RUN reports: RUN-fec2442f-7d58-41e5-a4d0-ee0dbd2a8d88.
