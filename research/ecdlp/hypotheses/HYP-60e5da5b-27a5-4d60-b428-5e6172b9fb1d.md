# HYP-60e5da5b-27a5-4d60-b428-5e6172b9fb1d — D1: no low-degree x-line orbit invariants

## Statement
D1 (speculative, from ECDLP-22): the action of scalar multiplication on x-coordinates (x ↦ x([m]Q), a degree-m² rational map on P¹ restricted to the x-values of a cyclic subgroup) admits no low-degree invariant: the least degree of a nonconstant polynomial invariant under the induced permutation of the M x-values equals M − c + 1 (c = number of cycles), as for a random permutation of the same cycle type; in particular no hidden low-degree structure is available to shortcut orbit computations.

## Status
Experimental null result (toy), low evidential value.
