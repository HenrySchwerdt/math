# HYP-242da418-4c1a-45b1-8132-8a2f00574272 — Flat spectra of Legendre sequences: Weil/Katz explanation

## Exact statement
For E/F_p with #E(F_p)=r prime, generator P and a nonconstant rational function g on E of small degree d (e.g. g=x, x+1, 2x, y), the normalized twisted sums X_c = r^{-1/2} * sum_{k=1}^{r-1} chi(g([k]P)) e(ck/r), c=1..r-1, satisfy |X_c| <= C(d) * sqrt(p/r) = O_d(1) (Weil-type bound for mixed character sums over E(F_p), group characters of E times a multiplicative character of g), so the periodogram |X_c|^2 has a bounded, non-exponential law; as p grows its distribution approaches a fixed law determined by the monodromy of the associated sheaf (not Exp(1)). This explains the flat-spectrum anomaly observed in ECDLP-7 E-B for chi(x), chi(y) sequences, and implies no information about k beyond what the bound allows.

## Provenance
PI conjecture after ECDLP-7 (RUN-6fce1db8) observed periodogram variance ~0.66 (vs 1 for random) for chi(x([k]P)), chi(y([k]P)), chi(x+1), chi(2x), not for parity/hash/chi(x^2+x+1)/chi(xy). Reproduced across two seeds and by PI rerun.

## What would invalidate it
max_c |X_c| growing like sqrt(log r) relative to sqrt(p/r) (random-like), or flatness for non-algebraic predicates, or an oracle bug.

## Status
Unproved conjecture; no literature verified yet.
