# HYP-f85d20a1-1a1c-4f2e-8d5c-abd77fe2907d — Exact uniformity of Smart quantity under independent lifts (N4)

## Exact statement
Let E/F_p with p not dividing #E(F_p), fix a lift of the curve mod p^2, and P of order r != p. For independent uniform lifts P~, Q~ (mod p^2) of P and Q = [k]P, the pair (psi(P~), psi(Q~)) is uniform on F_p^2 for every k, where psi(X~) = (-x/y)([#E] X~)/p mod p. For consistent lifts Q~ = [k]P~ one has psi(Q~) = k psi(P~) exactly. Hence psi carries information about k only through the choice of lifts, i.e. only when k is already used.
