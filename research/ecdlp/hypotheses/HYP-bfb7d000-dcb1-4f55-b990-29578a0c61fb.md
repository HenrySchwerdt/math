# HYP-bfb7d000-dcb1-4f55-b990-29578a0c61fb — Inventor H: fixed-curve precomputation with comparisons/bits stays on S T^2 ~ r

Status: completed; proof status: experimental; outcome: refuted.

## Question, selection rationale and inputs
Inventor H (ECDLP-56, fixed-curve precomputation with comparisons and bits): negative, no S T^2/r falling with r out of sample (pre-registered rule not met). E1 sorted x-table + interpolation search = BSGS in disguise (saves probes, not group ops); S T^2/r at 2^16..2^32: S=r^{1/3}: 1.1e2-1.4e5, r^{1/2}: 44-3.6e3, r^{2/3}: 74-141; 16,200 queries verified. E2 greedy comparison decision trees on bits/thresholds of x(Q), x(Q+-P) (15-4095 nodes, 5 sizes x 3 curves): held-out accuracy 0.059-0.065 vs chance 0.0625, 0/90 cells leak; planted 2-bit leak detected (0.25) at every size. E3 curve-tuned walk tables: S T^2/r = 3.9 (tuned) vs 4.5 (generic) at 2^32, ratio 0.86 [0.69,1.06]; post-hoc 24-32-bit slope -0.049 did not replicate on 9 fresh curves (+0.004 [-0.057,0.064]). Obstacle: information about k enters only via exact collisions with advice points (probability ~ S L^2/r, constant ratio); tuning pseudorandom step labels gains only extreme-value constants (~12%); a non-random contracting step function would be needed and none of 7 families worked.
