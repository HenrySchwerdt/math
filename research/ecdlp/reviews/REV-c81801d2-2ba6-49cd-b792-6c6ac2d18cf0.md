## Addendum check
The statuses (formally verified with review pending / proof attempt / experimental) are distinct, correct and repeated in the limitations. The Lean quote is exact. The cost corollary is explicitly marked as not formalized and is confined to the restricted model; the O(n log n) target is stated as unaffected. I found no overclaiming.

Non-blocking residuals:
- **R1. The item count is inconsistent.** The abstract (l.39-40), l.65 and l.254 say "two" addendum items, but the addendum has three: (i)-(iii). The abstract also omits C*.
- **R2. l.54-55 contradicts l.57-58.** l.54-55 says the bound says "nothing about lower bounds on the cost of evaluating it". l.57-58 then gives a logarithmic lower bound on the multiplication count. Suggested rewording: "nothing beyond a logarithmic lower bound on …".
- **R3. The constant-factor claim at l.68-69 is too broad.** "Theorems B-D determine the minimal interpolation degree up to a constant factor" holds for B and C. D is a partial-agreement count, not a minimal-degree statement on a range.
- **R4. DOIs and l.259 wording.**
  - `\bibliographystyle{plain}` drops the `doi` field, so the PDF bibliography shows no DOIs although l.251 lists DOIs among the checked data. Use `plainurl` or put the DOI in `note`.
  - At l.259, "a constant of F (free)" could be read as constants costing nothing. In Lean every op, including `const` and `var`, counts toward L. Say "an arbitrary constant of F".
- **R5. The L2 statement (l.281) omits two conditions present in the ledger mechanism.** It does not state that D ≢ 0, and it does not state that the zeros come from Σcᵢf(k+i)=0 (f of degree ≤ 1). The paper presents L2 as recorded and unreviewed, so this is presentation only.
- The bib note for LW02 ("as reported by REV-2b383b9b") can now also cite this independent confirmation.
