## Findings (initial, before reading author notes)

### Blocking
- **B1. The axiom evidence path is wrong for Theorems C and D (l.205–207).** The paper says `#print axioms` on all three declarations reports the standard axioms, and cites a single evidence file, `RUN-62dbda7c-...-command-b585f279-...evidence`. That file is the run of `Round7Axioms.lean`, and its output lists only `degree_bound_short_weierstrass` (Theorem B) and the CurveX helper lemmas. The axiom outputs for C and D are in `...-command-5da55f17-...evidence` (Round8Axioms) and `...-command-ca0a1a26-...evidence` (Round9Axioms), which the PRF records cite.
  - The underlying fact is true: the PRF evidence shows it, and my rerun confirms it.
  - It is blocking anyway. The paper's only claimed contribution is the machine-checked formalization, and PAPERS.md requires exact evidence in the formal-verification section.
  - Fix: cite all three evidence files, one per theorem, or a single new exec-recorded `#print axioms` run covering all three declarations.

### Non-blocking
- **N1. Implicit binders and the xCoord convention are not stated (l.174).**
  - The quotes are verbatim, which I confirmed. Their binders `{F : Type*} [Field F] [DecidableEq F]` come from `variable` lines, `Affine` means `WeierstrassCurve.Affine` under `open WeierstrassCurve`, and `F[X]` means `Polynomial F`.
  - `xCoord` is defined as 0 at the point at infinity (CurveX.lean l.47–52). This is harmless here because every point evaluated is k•P with 1≤k<r, but a referee would want one sentence saying so.
  - Recommendation: add one sentence covering both points, and map each declaration to its file. Theorems C and D live in namespace `CurveX` but are declared in LinearInterp.lean and PartialAgreement.lean; l.169–170 and l.197 do not say this.
- **N2. "No existence statement" undersells a trivial fact (l.49, l.236).**
  - The nodes x(kP) are distinct by injectivity, so Lagrange interpolation always gives g with deg g ≤ N−1. For B that is deg g ≤ (r−3)/2. PRF-34538f75 itself uses this to argue non-vacuity.
  - So the lower bounds determine the minimal degree up to a factor of about 3. They are linear in r and in no way surprising.
  - Saying "we do not claim interpolating polynomials exist, nor give a matching upper bound" is technically true but misleading.
  - Recommendation: state the trivial upper bound explicitly and phrase the result as determining the minimal interpolation degree up to a constant factor. This improves honesty, not the claim.
- **N3. The interpretation sentence on degree versus cost is imprecise (l.50–51).** "A polynomial of degree d over F_p can be evaluated with a number of field operations that is not controlled by d in general" is ambiguous. Horner's rule gives an upper bound of O(d). The relevant point is that high degree does not imply high evaluation cost; for example, X^{p−2} costs O(log p) multiplications. Recommendation: reword along these lines. The conclusion (no bit-complexity consequence) is correct and stated clearly at l.37, l.52–54, l.234 and l.239.
- **N4. Theorem numbering is mixed.** The environments print as "Theorem 1 (B …)", "Theorem 2 (C …)" and "Theorem 3 (D …)", and `\ref` produces "Theorem 3" (l.108). The prose meanwhile uses "Theorem B/C/D" (l.125, l.146, l.151, l.154, l.200–203), and "Theorem A" is not a theorem of the paper (Remark 5). Recommendation: use one scheme, for example `\newtheorem*` with explicit names, or number throughout.
- **N5. The bibliography is incomplete but correct where present (refs.bib; l.229–230).**
  - All three entries are real publications with correct titles, venues, volumes, pages and years (see the table below).
  - Author first names (Tanja Lange, Arne Winterhof, Takakazu Satoh) and DOIs are missing.
  - The Sat05 note calls the year 2006 "unverified", and l.229–230 repeats this; Crossref confirms 2006.
  - Recommendation: complete the entries and drop the "unverified" wording.
- **N6. Relevant prior work in the ledger is omitted (Section 6, l.220–226).**
  - The ledger source records (RUN-4b283c25, RUN-73ace464, RUN-e7c9feff) list:
    - Coppersmith–Shparlinski, J. Cryptology 13 (2000) 339–360, the foundational polynomial-interpolation-of-DL paper;
    - Kiltz–Winterhof, Discrete Appl. Math. 154 (2006) 326–336;
    - Kiltz–Winterhof, Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 69 (2004), whose Section 5 (on EC Diffie–Hellman) was actually read;
    - Shparlinski's 2003 book.
  - The introduction refers to "interpolation results" (l.46) but cites only LW02, LW03 and Sat05.
  - Recommendation: cite at least Coppersmith–Shparlinski, verified here via Crossref, and Kiltz–Winterhof (2006), which already has a Crossref record.
- **N7. Novelty wording for Theorem C is softer than the ledger.** The ledger record HYP-42f91290 says "Likely implied by / weaker than Lange–Winterhof-type results … NOT claimed novel". The paper says "may be known or weaker" (l.36, l.224) and "probably not novel" (l.226). This is acceptable, and the "unsettled" status is clearly stated, but l.226 could quote the ledger's stronger "likely implied by".
- **N8. Minor issues in the proofs (none affects correctness).**
  - l.136: "the third degree 2m" is wrong when c=0 (Theorem C uses c=0, where the term vanishes); write "degree ≤ 2m".
  - l.142: "Q=kP≠P0 in x-coordinate, x(Q)≠x0" is redundant. Also say that Q itself is affine.
  - l.127: "(formal) roots" should say that g(s₊)+g(s₋) is defined as the symmetric polynomial expression in e₁,e₂.
  - l.81: "2≠0≠3" is non-transitive shorthand; write "2≠0 and 3≠0".
  - Otherwise I found no errors:
    - The proofs check out, including the root counts (r−5)/2, N−2 and |C_j|, the affine and distinctness conditions, and the constant-g cases.
    - The identities (eq:sum) and (eq:prod) match the standard x-only formulas; the sign convention s± with v∓y₀ is correct.
    - The F_p Hasse step at l.102–105 is valid (3p+1>2√p), correctly flagged as not formalized, and correctly supersedes the false "j≤N<p" argument.
- **N9. The ledger ID is truncated (l.85, l.119).** HYP IDs are given as 8-hex prefixes, which is acceptable for an internal report, but the PDF also hyphen-breaks some of them ("PRF34538f75" at the line break near l.166; "HYP-adb581" is truncated in pdftotext). Recommendation: use `\path` or `\mbox` for the IDs.
